About Curious2Study

Curious2Study is a free educational resource designed to help high school and lower-division undergraduate students develop effective study skills. Our mission is to teach you how to learn, not just how to memorize facts.

Each course begins with video overviews created from OpenStax textbooks. We are continuously expanding our library to include study guides, practice problems, and additional resources to help you master the material and develop lasting learning strategies.
