# Curious2Study

## Mission
Helping students master the fundamentals by teaching **how to learn**, not just what to memorize.

## About This Project
After years in the classroom, I've noticed the biggest obstacle students face isn't the material itself—it's not knowing how to study it. Curious2Study provides structured learning resources designed to build genuine understanding and effective study skills.

These materials are for high school and lower-division undergraduate students struggling with foundational courses.

## How to Use These Resources
- Start with a subject project below
- Follow videos organized by chapter or topic
- Use study guides and materials to reinforce concepts
- Focus on understanding the "why," not just memorizing the "what"
